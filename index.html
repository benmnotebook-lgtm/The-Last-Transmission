01 / THE GAME

THE GAME

THE LAST TRANSMISSION is an open-world military mystery game focused on exploration, investigation, communication and consequences.

Instead of simply following mission markers, players have to observe the world, collect information and understand what is actually happening around them.

The island continues to exist even when the player is not directly involved. Events happen, factions act and information changes over time.

GENRE OPEN-WORLD / MILITARY / MYSTERY / ACTION
PLATFORM PC
ENGINE UNREAL ENGINE 5.8.1
RELEASE 2030
02 / THE WORLD

THE WORLD

The game takes place on a remote island inspired by the landscapes of Tanaga Island in Alaska.

The island is isolated from the outside world and contains military facilities, abandoned locations, communication infrastructure, settlements and areas that have been left untouched for years.

The environment is not simply a background. Locations, objects, signals and events can contain information about what happened.

01 REMOTE Far away from the normal world.
02 UNSTABLE Events can change the state of the island.
03 ALIVE The world continues without the player.
04 UNKNOWN Not everything you hear is true.
03 / THE STORY

THE STORY

Something happened on the island.

Communications were interrupted. Information disappeared. People stopped responding. Different groups began telling different versions of the same events.

The player is not given the complete truth at the beginning. Instead, the story is reconstructed through transmissions, conversations, documents, locations and events.

Some information can be incomplete. Some can be outdated. Some can contradict other information.

THE CORE LOOP EXPLORE → OBSERVE → COLLECT INFORMATION → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → ACT → EXPERIENCE CONSEQUENCES
